## 2. Market

- India geotextiles market ≈ **USD 330 M (2025) → USD 671 M by 2034, ~8% CAGR** (IMARC). Wider geosynthetics estimates run up to USD 1.8 B.
- Drivers: Bharatmala and highway programmes, railways/metro, Smart Cities, river and coastal protection.
- **Non-woven holds the larger share** of Indian geotextiles — exactly Manas's core product.
- **Regulation is a selling point:** the Ministry of Textiles' Geotextiles Quality Control Orders make BIS certification mandatory for covered geotextiles (IS 16391 separation, IS 16362 stabilisation, IS 16090 protection, IS 16392 erosion control). Buyers now search for this.

The realistic path is to win the long-tail and commercial terms above (which convert far better), build authority, and climb toward the head term over 6–12 months.

## 4. What the new site does for SEO

- One page per product, application and question — each with a unique title, meta description, H1 and canonical URL.
- Structured data: Organization + Manufacturer, LocalBusiness (factory), WebSite, Product, Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, WebApplication.
- Auto-generated Open Graph images for every page (WhatsApp/LinkedIn previews).
- 301/308 redirects from every old `.html` URL so existing rankings and backlinks carry over.
- Static pages, small JS, no heavy 3D — fast Core Web Vitals on Indian mobile networks.
- XML sitemap, robots.txt, web manifest, `en-IN` language.
- Internal linking mesh: product ↔ application ↔ guide ↔ calculator ↔ quote.

## 5. Conversion hooks

- **Interactive hero:** drag "years of traffic" and watch a road fail without geotextile — explains the product in 5 seconds.
- **Free calculator** → pre-filled quote with area and product.
- **WhatsApp button** on every page (dominant B2B channel in India).
- Quote buttons on every product/application page; forms pre-filled from context.

## 6. Off-site actions (must be done by the client — the site alone won't rank)

1. **Google Search Console**: verify domain, submit sitemap, request indexing (add token in `src/app/layout.tsx`).
2. **Google Business Profile** for both head office and factory — biggest lever for "geotextile manufacturer near me / in Delhi / Haryana".
3. Make NAP (name, address, phone) identical on IndiaMART, TradeIndia, JustDial, LinkedIn and the website.
4. **Proof content:** add BIS licence numbers, ISO certificates, plant photos/video, 3–5 named project case studies. This is the single biggest content gap.
5. Backlinks: trade press (Techtextiles & Nonwovens already covered Manas), Indian Geosynthetics Society membership, supplier listings on L&T-SuFin and contractor portals.
6. Publish one new guide per month (e.g. geotextile for landfill CQA, geo bag sizes, IS 16391 test methods).
7. Reviews on Google Business Profile from contractors.
